Headphone EQ redistributes modified subsets of D1 (KU100), D2 (KEMAR), and
H10. The already diffuse-field-equalized 48 kHz 256-tap measurements are
subsampled in direction, padded, and time-aligned for interpolation, with
original per-ear peak times retained. No additional headphone compensation
from SADIE II is included. See provenance.json for exact source URLs and
SHA-256 digests, and Scripts/prepare_hrtf.py in the source distribution.
The University of York does not endorse Headphone EQ.
